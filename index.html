Our Mission

Built by a carpenter,
for everyday people

Jesus was called a carpenter — a builder who worked with His hands among ordinary people. In that same spirit, Carpenter Bible Commentary is built to make God's Word accessible to everyone.

We believe you shouldn't need a theology degree to understand the Bible. Our commentary is written in plain, conversational English — the kind of language you'd use talking to a friend over coffee.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or you've been a believer for decades, Carpenter Bible Commentary meets you where you are and helps you go deeper.
