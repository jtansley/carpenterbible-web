Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

Overview

Carpenter Bible Commentary ("the App") is designed to respect your privacy. The App runs entirely offline on your device. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location or device identifiers
  • Your reading history, bookmarks, highlights, or notes
  • Analytics, usage data, or tracking information

All of your data — including bookmarks, highlights, notes, and prayers — is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time premium purchase processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play. These purchases are handled by RevenueCat, a third-party service that manages purchase validation.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous transaction identifier from the app store to verify your purchase. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any personally identifiable information. We do not have the ability to identify you through this process.

For details on how RevenueCat handles data, please see their Privacy Policy.

No Account Required

The App does not use any authentication system. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. You simply download the App and start reading.

Offline Operation

The App is designed to work 100% offline. All Bible text and commentary content is included in the App download. No internet connection is required to use any feature of the App.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, the App is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
